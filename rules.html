Rules & regulations

A quick guide for athletes and coaches: how scoring works, what is illegal, and the time limits for each division. For the full official text, see the ADCC rules & regulations.

Tournament basics

  • The championship follows a tournament format. Only the winner advances to the next round.
  • Gi/kimono and wrestling shoes are optional.
  • Slippery substances on the body or clothing are prohibited.
  • The mat area is 8 × 8 meters.
  • There is no cage or fence.

Scoring positions

Mount

2pts
  • Both knees must be touching the floor.
  • Both knees must be below the shoulder line and the top of the hip line.
  • Half back must be on the floor.
  • Reverse mount also scores.
  • If one or both of the opponent’s hands are trapped under your legs, it still scores.
  • In an Absolute fight with a size difference that prevents a fighter from touching both knees to the mat, referees decide whether sufficient control exists.

Back mount

3pts
  • Both hooks and a body triangle are acceptable.
  • Hooks must not be over either shoulder.
  • If one hook is removed or the body triangle is released for more than 3 seconds, and then the hooks or body triangle are re-established for 3 seconds or more, points are awarded again.
  • If one or both of the opponent’s hands are trapped under your legs, it still scores.

Passing the guard

3pts
  • Control is required, with at least 50% of the opponent’s back on the mat.
  • If passing the guard directly leads to mount or knee on stomach in less than 3 seconds, only guard-pass points are awarded.

Knee on stomach

2pts
  • Either the inside or outside knee can score.
  • The knee must be in the middle of the stomach, not on the chest or sides of the stomach.
  • The opponent cannot be on their side.
  • Half back must be on the floor.
  • If the knee is removed for 3 seconds or more and then re-established for 3 seconds or more, points are awarded again.
  • The position of the other leg is irrelevant. Both knees may be on the mat.

Clean sweep

4pts
  • The athletes face each other and the bottom athlete moves to the top, ending outside the guard.
  • More than 50% of the opponent’s back must be on the floor.
  • The position must be established for 3 seconds or more.
  • The athlete must not be in danger of submission.

Sweep

2pts
  • The athletes face each other and the bottom athlete moves to the top.
  • The position ends in guard or half guard.
  • The position must be established for 3 seconds or more.
  • The athlete must not be in danger of submission.

Clean takedown

4pts
  • End outside the guard.
  • At least 50% of the opponent’s back must be on the mat.
  • The athlete must not be in danger of submission.

Takedown

2pts
  • End in guard or half guard.
  • The opponent’s butt or back must remain on the mat for 3 seconds.
  • If the opponent threatens a submission, points are awarded only after escaping the submission threat and solidifying the position for 3 seconds.

Important scoring rules

  • Reversals are considered sweeps.
  • Reversing from bottom to top while held in side control or mount can score 2 points if ending in guard and 4 points if ending in side control.
  • Every sweep must be completed in one continuous motion.
  • Sweep points are awarded only if the fighter initiates the sweep, not if they end up on top while defending an opponent’s attack.
  • When multiple positions are changed, points are awarded only for the position established for 3 seconds or more.
  • Each position must be established for at least 3 seconds, with no submission danger, for points to be awarded.
  • Passing the guard directly to mount or knee on stomach awards only guard-pass points.

Penalties

A minus point may be given for:

  • Voluntarily jumping guard or going from standing to a non-standing position by any means and remaining down for 3 seconds or more.
  • Disengaging from contact, backing away, and avoiding engagement.
  • Holding without doing anything else.
  • Passivity.
  • Repeatedly fleeing the mat to escape a submission or disengage from the fight.
  • Bad language or bad behavior by a fighter or coach.
  • Disobeying the referee’s commands.

Passivity

  • A passive fighter receives two warnings before a minus point.
  • The referee announces: “WARNING PASSIVITY”.
  • After the first minus point, continued passivity results in further minus points without additional warnings.
  • If a fighter is very passive during the first half of a regular fight, when there are no minuses, the referee may warn them then and issue a negative point when the second half begins.

When a minus point is given

  • The referee stops the action and informs the athlete and coach.

Fixed fights

  • If teammates arrange a fixed fight, both are disqualified from the tournament.

Illegal techniques

  • Attempting any illegal technique results in immediate disqualification.

How to win

A competitor wins when

  • The opponent submits by tapping the leg or arm, or verbally saying “I give up” or “Maita”.
  • The referee determines that a competitor cannot defend themselves or that their life is in danger.
  • The opponent is disqualified by the referee for breaking the rules twice.

Overtime

  • If the fight ends without either fighter scoring points, the referees decide the winner according to fighter dominance.

Rules by division

Professional

Legal techniques

  • Any kind of choke, except using the hand to close the windpipe.
  • Any armbar.
  • Any shoulder lock.
  • Any wrist lock.
  • Any leg lock.
  • Any ankle lock.

Illegal techniques

  • Full Nelson.
  • Crucifix.
  • Neck cranks that trap both shoulders and put downward pressure on the neck.
  • Spiking an opponent on their head during a takedown attempt.
  • Spiking an opponent’s head when the opponent is on your back.
  • Rolling forward while someone is on your back.
  • Striking of any kind.
  • Eye gouging or fish hooking.
  • Grabbing the ears.
  • Hair pulling.
  • Holding fewer than 4 fingers or fewer than 5 toes.
  • Thumbing.
  • Scratching or pinching.
  • Punching of any kind.
  • Kicking of any kind.
  • Biting.
  • Squeezing or applying pressure to the groin.
  • Hands, knees, or elbows on the face.
  • Slippery substances on the body or clothing.
  • Crossface guillotine twisting the chin.
  • Gripping or holding the T-shirt or shorts.
  • Slamming, except when there is a submission hold.

Fight time limits

National championships

  • Qualifying rounds: 6 minutes.
  • Overtime: 3 minutes if tied with no advantage.
  • Finals: 8 minutes.
  • Overtime: 4 minutes if tied with no advantage.

Trials

  • Qualifying rounds: 8 minutes.
  • Overtime: 4 minutes if tied with no advantage.
  • Finals: 10 minutes.
  • Overtime: 5 minutes if tied with no advantage.

World championship

  • Qualifying rounds: 10 minutes.
  • Overtime: 5 minutes if tied with no advantage.
  • Finals: 20 minutes.
  • Overtime: 10 minutes if tied with no advantage.

Fight time limits

Qualifying rounds

  • Total fight time: 6 minutes.
  • First 3 minutes: no positive points.
  • Positive points begin after the first 3 minutes and continue through the end of the fight and overtime.
  • If tied with no advantage: 3-minute overtime.
  • Maximum 1 overtime per fight.

Finals

  • Total fight time: 8 minutes.
  • First 4 minutes: no positive points. Negative points apply from the beginning.
  • Positive points begin after the first 4 minutes and continue through the end of the fight and overtime.
  • If tied with no advantage: 4-minute overtime.
  • Maximum 1 overtime per fight.

Fight time limits & safety

Qualifying rounds

  • Total fight time: 5 minutes.
  • First 2.5 minutes: no points.
  • Points begin after the first 2.5 minutes and continue through the end of the fight and overtime.
  • If tied with no advantage: 2.5-minute overtime.
  • Maximum 1 overtime per fight.

Finals

  • Total fight time: 6 minutes.
  • First 3 minutes: no positive points. Negative points apply from the beginning.
  • Positive points begin after the first 3 minutes and continue through the end of the fight and overtime.
  • If tied with no advantage: 3-minute overtime.
  • Maximum 1 overtime per fight.

Referee safety

  • Referees will stop the fight if a submission is applied and is too dangerous for the fighter, even if the fighter has not submitted.

Other important rules

  • If you start a submission from mount, side control, or your opponent’s guard and move from top to bottom, the opponent does not receive sweep points.
  • If your opponent initiates a sweep and you attack a submission during the motion, but they escape and hold you down for 3 seconds, they receive sweep points.
  • If an opponent takes you down and you remain in turtle for more than 3 seconds, there are no takedown points.
  • Shooting for a takedown and pulling guard in less than 3 seconds results in a minus point.
  • Shooting for a takedown and going to turtle in less than 3 seconds to break the attack results in a minus point.
  • If both fighters are standing and one or both knees remain on the mat for more than 3 seconds, a minus point is given.
  • If you attempt a takedown actively for more than 3 seconds and then pull guard, there is no minus point.
  • If you go out of bounds, do not stop. Only the referee can stop the match.
  • When the referee stops the match out of bounds, the fight restarts in the center in the same position.
  • If standing without an established locked position, the fighters restart facing each other.
  • If a competitor has single or double grips before the referee says stop, the athletes resume the same position.
  • If an accidental eye poke or groin strike causes bleeding, the fighter gets 2 minutes to recover. If unable to continue, they lose the fight.
